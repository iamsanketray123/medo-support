YOUR QUESTIONS

Good to know

How do I manage a subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

The policy is available here and inside the app. For a privacy inquiry, email iamsanketray123@gmail.com.

Does contacting support send my records?

Support messages open in Mail and are sent only after you review them. Medo does not upload medication records with feedback.

For app support, not medical advice.

For questions about medicines or treatment, contact your healthcare professional. In a medical emergency, call your local emergency services.
